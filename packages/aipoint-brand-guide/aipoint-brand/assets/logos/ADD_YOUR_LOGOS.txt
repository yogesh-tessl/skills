AIPoint Logo Files
==================

Place your official AIPoint logo files in this folder:

Recommended files to include:
- aipoint-logo.png (primary logo, high resolution)
- aipoint-logo.svg (vector format for scaling)
- aipoint-logo-white.png (white version for dark backgrounds)
- aipoint-icon.png (icon/favicon version)

The included aipoint-logo.svg is a recreation based on visual reference.
Replace it with your official logo file for accurate branding.
